# ShotOnLoc

A community map of the real-world places where movies and TV series were filmed.

## Language

### Content

**Title**:
A movie or TV series, identified by its TMDB entry.
_Avoid_: Film, movie (when series are included), show, work

**Scene**:
A specific moment of a Title, pinned at the single place where it was filmed. The central marker on the map.
_Avoid_: Marker, pin, location, spot, filming location

**Scene Name**:
The short human label of a Scene, e.g. "Chase on the stairs".
_Avoid_: Scene title (clashes with Title)

**Visitor Notes**:
Practical hints for people visiting a Scene's place, such as opening hours or access restrictions.
_Avoid_: Tips, comments

**Photo**:
An image of a Scene's place taken by a user, uploaded to that Scene and owned by its uploader.
_Avoid_: Picture, image, media, still

**Video Link**:
A link to an externally hosted video attached to a Scene; videos are never uploaded.
_Avoid_: Video, clip

### Origin and quality

**Imported Scene**:
A Scene created from Wikidata rather than by a user, unconfirmed until a user claims it.
_Avoid_: Seed, auto scene, suggestion

**Claim**:
A user taking over an Imported Scene, becoming its creator and confirming it.
_Avoid_: Adopt, confirm

**Creator**:
The user who created or claimed a Scene; may edit its Scene Name, description and pin.
_Avoid_: Owner, author

### People and access

**User**:
A registered person with one or more login methods.
_Avoid_: Account, member

**Admin**:
A User with the right to moderate content and create Invite Codes.
_Avoid_: Moderator, superuser

**Invite Code**:
A single-use, time-limited code required to register.
_Avoid_: Invitation, token, referral

**Visit**:
A User's record of having been at a Scene's place in person.
_Avoid_: Check-in, like, been-there
